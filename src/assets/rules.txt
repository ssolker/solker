WOODSTOCK SOCCER - ADULT CO-ED LEAGUE OPERATIONS GUIDE

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION

Mission: Cultivating a culture of accountability and connection in the Friendly City. Our mission is to provide a fun, self-regulated sports environment built on the pillars of integrity and community.

CORE PHILOSOPHY:
Spirit of the Game: The league operates on a self-officiated model to encourage player accountability and lower operational overhead. I believe that when players take ownership of the rules, the game becomes more honest, fluid, and enjoyable for everyone involved.
Collaborative Competition: Every match is a joint effort between two teams to maintain a safe, fair, and welcoming environment. I prioritize open communication and social connection, ensuring that providing a fun, active, and social outlet for Woodstock remains the primary goal of every season.
2. LEAGUE ESSENTIALS: STRUCTURE, FEES, & SCHEDULING

This section details the most relevant logistics for players and teams, including fees, structure, and time commitment.

ROSTER & STRUCTURE
Match Format: 6v6 (5 outfield players + 1 Goalkeeper).
Ten per roster
Gender Minimum: A minimum of 1 female player must be on the field at all times. The goalkeeper does not count toward this requirement.
Captains/Rep: Each team must designate one representative to manage team communication and oversee all game-day reporting, timekeeping, and delegation duties; confirmed Captains will receive "Captain’s Kit" with further instructions and equipment prior to the season.
Game Time: Matches kick off at 6:30 PM EST, featuring two 20-minute halves separated by a 5-minute halftime break.
Game Day: Each team is scheduled for two games per night
SEASON STRUCTURE & FEES
Season Length: Seasons run in 8-Week Blocks (e.g., May/June or July/August).
Player Fees: $50 CAD per player for the 8-week season.
Captains Fees: To recognize the time dedicated to team management and reporting, all registration fees for designated Captains are waived.
TBD:  10 per roster means $500 per team. Guest players do not pay if replacing someone who has paid. Part timers get a discount.
3. CAPTAIN ESSENTIALS & MATCH DAY DUTIES

This section outlines the mandatory roles and responsibilities for all team Captains.

Reporting & Timekeeping
Self-Officiating: There are no referees. Players call their own fouls.
The 30-Second Rule: If Captains cannot resolve a dispute within 30 seconds, play resets with an indirect free kick for the defending team at the spot of the foul.
Timekeeping: Both Captains are responsible for ensuring a "Timekeeper" is designated to manage the two 20-minute halves and the 5-minute halftime break.
Post-Game Reporting: Captains are required to submit the electronic Game Day & Spirit Form immediately after each game.
Waivers: Mandatory to be signed before playing for every participant.
4. OFFICIAL 6V6 LEAGUE RULES
The Start: Initial possession is determined by rock-paper-scissors. Kickoff can go in any direction. Possession and sides swap at halftime.
Offsides: There are no offside penalties in this 6v6 format.
No Sliding: Zero-tolerance policy. Players must stay on their feet. Any slide (intentional or not) results in an indirect free kick for the opponent.
Restarts:
Sidelines: All balls out of play are Kick-ins (not throw-ins). Kick-ins are indirect.
Goal Kicks: Can be kicked or thrown by the goalie.
Distance: Opponents must provide 5 yards (5 big steps) of space on all restarts.
Free Kicks: All fouls result in an Indirect kick (must touch another player before scoring) except for Penalty Kicks.
Penalty Kicks: Taken from 8 meters for fouls committed inside the box that stop a clear scoring chance.
Goalkeeper: May use hands inside the designated box. The "Back Pass" rule applies—goalies cannot pick up a ball intentionally kicked to them by a teammate.
5. FRIENDLY CITY SPIRIT SCORE

The Spirit Score is a fundamental pillar of the “Friendly City” approach. It ensures that on-field conduct and collaborative communication are valued just as highly as athletic skill. The rubric and scores will be shared with the team Captains.

Why Spirit Matters
Standings & Tie-Breakers: In the event of a tie in the standings, the team with the higher Spirit Average will automatically take the higher seed.
